Delete Your Account & Data

You can request deletion of your Impact Konnect account and the personal data associated with it at any time, using either of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

Open the Impact Konnect app, go to Profile → Quick Actions → Delete Account, confirm, and re-enter your password. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us from the address associated with your account and we will delete it within a reasonable time.

Request Deletion by Email

What gets deleted

  • Your user account (sign-in credentials)
  • Your profile record (name, email, role, assigned state)
  • Your saved favorites

What is retained

Stakeholder directory records you may have created or edited as an administrator are organizational program data, not your personal data, and are not deleted along with your account. Some activity/audit logs may be retained in de-identified form for security and program reporting purposes, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

Timeline

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are processed within 30 days.
