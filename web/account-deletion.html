Impact Konnect Account Deletion

Users may delete their account directly from within the Impact Konnect mobile application by navigating to:

Profile → Delete Account

Alternatively, users may request account deletion by contacting their organization administrator or by emailing:

support@davhub.com.ng

What happens when an account is deleted?

When an account deletion request is confirmed:

  • The user account is permanently deleted.
  • Personal profile information is removed.
  • Authentication credentials are deleted.
  • Any data that must be retained for legal, security, or audit purposes will be retained only for as long as required by applicable laws or organizational policies.

Account deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days.
